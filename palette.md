# Jezabel site palette

Sampled on 10-16-26 from two references: the Romeo Santos and Prince Royce *Better Late Than Never* cover (sunset on dark water) and the *Jezabel* lyric-video title card (white serif title over flames on black). Region averages and an 8-color median cut, not eyeballed. One accent does all the action work.

| Token | Hex | Role | Where it was sampled |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ground` | `#080403` | page background | title card background `#060000`, cover bottom-left `#080605` |
| `--sea` | `#1b1411` | surfaces, the ring band | cover median cut, darkest brown |
| `--swell` | `#43261a` | mid water, hover surfaces | cover water, middle band |
| `--ember` | `#5b1613` | deep red corner of the hero canvas | cover bottom-right |
| `--copper` | `#974a29` | water just under the horizon, sky mid | cover median cut, second largest share |
| `--amber` | `#bf834c` | the accent: hover fills, active underlines, focus rings | cover sky top band |
| `--glow` | `#f1bf7d` | numerals, dates, the sun column | cover sun column |
| `--sun` | `#fefbdf` | hottest streaks on the water | brightest 200 px of the cover's sun |
| `--sand` | `#faf6ef` | body type | warmed from the title card's white so paragraphs do not glare |
| `--title` | `#fefefe` | the JEZABEL title only | title card type, brightest 400 px |
| `--dune` | `#a08573` | muted type | lifted from the title card smoke `#8f7565` for 5.6:1 contrast on the ground |

Also on record from the samples, unused as tokens: cover sky low `#ab7041`, cover highlights `#ecae6a` and `#b76a37` (the water streaks run between them), flame core `#a55d23`.

Type: Bodoni Moda for the title and every numeral (the title card's high-contrast serif), Jost for the tracked caps (the cover's artist line) and body copy.
